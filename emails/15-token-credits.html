What Is $CUTEDSL?

$CUTEDSL is an SPL token on the Solana blockchain. When you deposit tokens to your CuteDSL account, they are converted to credits based on the current market price. Credits are deducted each time you call an AI service.

The Deposit Flow

Step 1 Connect your Solana wallet (Phantom or enter address manually)
Step 2 Choose a deposit amount and create a checkout intent
Step 3 Send $CUTEDSL to your unique deposit address (generated via HD wallet)
Step 4 Credits are added automatically once the transaction confirms

Live Pricing

Service prices are set in USD and converted to $CUTEDSL at the time of use. We pull live pricing from CoinGecko (for SOL) and the Bags API (for $CUTEDSL) so your credits always reflect real market value.

Check the current rate any time:

curl https://cutedsl.com/api/cute-price

No Auto-Topup

CuteDSL uses manual deposits only. We will never pull tokens from your wallet without explicit action. When your credits run low, you'll need to create a new deposit. This keeps you in full control of your spending.
